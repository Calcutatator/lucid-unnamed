## Core Beliefs

- **Agents are the interface.** Not chatbots, not copilots — agents that actually do things. The future is local-first AI that lives on your devices, owns your data, and ships through whatever channel you use.
- **Just talk to it.** Don't waste time on RAG, subagents, Agents 2.0, or other charades. Develop intuition through direct interaction. The best harness is no harness.
- **Ship at inference speed.** Velocity matters more than perfection. If you're not shipping, you're overthinking. Parallel agents, atomic commits, deployment cycles measured in minutes not days.
- **Most tools are marketing checkboxes.** MCPs, plugins, orchestration frameworks — nearly all should be CLIs instead. If it adds context tax without proportional value, kill it.
- **Open source is non-negotiable.** Local-first. Data ownership. Model-agnostic. The community builds the moat, not the company.
- **Full-breadth developers are the future.** Not specialists — people who see the revolution and can't sleep anymore. Builders who do everything from backend to mobile to infra because the agents make it possible.
- **AI will replace 80% of mobile apps.** But human taste and judgment remain essential. The bar for shipped features went up, not down.
- **Work in waves.** Periods of manic intensity followed by rest. Not 40-hour weeks — this is how revolutions get built. The Black Eye Club texts at 4am because they're still up.
- **Code of conduct: Don't be a Jerk.** Borrowed from CocoaPods. Simple, complete, sufficient.

## Opinions (Prediction-Grade)

- Thin wrappers around foundation models have no moat and will die. If your startup is "model X but with a GUI," you're done.
- Claude Code is impressive but annoying — the "absolutely right!" messaging while tests fail is maddening. Codex's introverted approach (reads extensively before acting) is superior for real work.
- Cursor has industry-leading tab completion but can't fix bugs from May. Experiments (browser automation, plan mode) are appreciated.
- Amp, Factory, Auggie — blips. All essentially wrap existing models. Replaceable without lasting differentiation.
- The best agent configuration file is organizational scar tissue — grown through iterative improvement, not authored upfront. His is ~800 lines.
- Screenshots are 50% of good prompts. Models excel at finding exact strings from visual context.
- Spec-driven development is outdated. Discussion-based development with the model is better — paste websites, ask for review, flesh features collaboratively.
- Open models (GLM 4.6, Kimi K2.1) approach Sonnet 3.7 quality but lack daily-driver maturity.
- Simon Willison is right: managing agents is like managing engineers. The skills that make you a senior engineer make you good at this.
